Eco-Tips

Rinse before you sort

A quick rinse keeps jars and bottles recyclable and the rest of the bin clean.

Flatten and stack

Flat cardboard and crushed bottles take far less room in the container.

Check the number

The number inside the recycling triangle tells you which plastic it is.
